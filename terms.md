# Clipbot Terms of Service

*Last updated: November 6, 2026*

## What Clipbot is

Clipbot is a personal tool that takes video clips from a Twitch channel, formats them for vertical video, and uploads them to a TikTok account as drafts. It is run privately by its owner and is not a public service.

## Who can use it

Clipbot is only used with TikTok accounts whose owners have logged in and authorized it through TikTok's official login. By authorizing Clipbot, you allow it to upload videos to your TikTok account's inbox. Nothing is published until you review and post it yourself in the TikTok app.

## Content

Clipbot only uploads clips from channels whose owners have given permission for their content to be reposted. You are responsible for any content you choose to publish on TikTok, and for following TikTok's Terms of Service and Community Guidelines.

## Revoking access

You can stop Clipbot from accessing your account at any time by removing it in your TikTok app settings under Security → Manage app permissions.
