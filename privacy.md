# Clipbot Privacy Policy

*Last updated: November 6, 2026*

## What Clipbot collects

When you authorize Clipbot through TikTok's login, it receives:

- **An access token and refresh token**, which let it upload videos to your account.
- **Basic profile information** (the `user.info.basic` scope), such as your TikTok open ID and display name.

Clipbot does not collect your password, email address, contacts, messages, location or viewing history.

## How it is used

The tokens are used only to upload videos to your TikTok account's inbox as drafts and to check whether an upload succeeded. Basic profile information is used only to confirm which account is connected.

## Where it is stored

Tokens are stored in a local file on the computer that runs Clipbot. They are not uploaded to any server, database or third party. Clipbot also keeps a local record of which clips it has already uploaded, so it doesn't upload the same clip twice.

## Sharing

Clipbot does not sell, rent or share your data with anyone. The only services it communicates with are TikTok (to upload videos) and Twitch (to find and download clips).

## Deleting your data

You can revoke Clipbot's access at any time in your TikTok app settings under Security → Manage app permissions. This makes the stored tokens useless. You can also ask the owner to delete the local token file by contacting the address below.
